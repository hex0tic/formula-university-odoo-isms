# Form — Formula University

Form appears in the ERP and on signed-in portal pages. Its automatic greeting
appears once per user per local calendar day; ask “Өнөөдрийн ажил” in chat
to reopen the brief. The gear menu sets three sizes or hides the character.
The compact Form button restores it. Size and visibility are saved on the
signed-in account. Chat remains in memory for the open page.

The local assistant answers from the signed-in account's own assigned tasks,
activities, due incidents and today's classes. Student and teacher ownership
uses the campus models and their record rules. If the separate Formula ISMS
Copilot is installed, ISMS members can also ask about readable ISMS records
using its local search. Form does not call a third-party model or send ERP data
outside Odoo.
